About

Built from observation, layering, and slow transformation.

Based between New York and Lisbon, Ryan Randall draws from coastal light, urban architecture, and the emotional residue of fleeting moments. Each painting begins with a field of color and a fragment of memory, then evolves through multiple passes until the surface feels excavated and alive.

His practice spans painting, installation, and material studies, exploring how movement and stillness can coexist in a single image. The resulting works are intimate in scale but expansive in feeling—inviting the viewer to slow down and read the atmosphere as carefully as the form.
